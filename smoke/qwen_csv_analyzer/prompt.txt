Build a small CSV sales analyzer from input/sales.csv.

The finished tool should include analyze_sales.py, runnable from the project root as:
python analyze_sales.py --input input/sales.csv --json report.json --markdown report.md

It should produce report.json with total_revenue, order_count, units_by_product, revenue_by_region, top_product_by_revenue, and date_range. Money values should be rounded to 2 decimals.

It should also produce report.md with a title, summary bullets, a revenue-by-region table, and a top-product section. README.md should document Usage, Outputs, and Assumptions.

Do not ask questions. Implement and verify the tool, outputs, and documentation.
